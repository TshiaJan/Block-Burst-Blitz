# Privacy Policy for Block Burst Blitz

**Last Updated:** September 17, 2026

Welcome to **Block Burst Blitz** ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and providing a safe, enjoyable gaming experience. This Privacy Policy outlines our practices regarding data collection, use, and disclosure when you install and play Block Burst Blitz.

---

### 1. Information We Do Not Collect
- **No Personal Accounts:** Block Burst Blitz does not require you to create an account, log in, or provide personal details such as your name, email address, physical address, or phone number.
- **No Contact Access or Media Scanning:** The application does not request permission to access your personal contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or file storage beyond necessary application sandboxing.

---

### 2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device
To maintain your game state and user preferences, Block Burst Blitz saves minimal gameplay data directly on your device using local storage (`localStorage`):
- **Level Progress & High Scores:** Your current puzzle level, best level achieved, total score, and stars won.
- **In-Game Tokens:** Pretend token balance earned through gameplay achievements or optional rewarded interactions.
- **Player Settings:** Preferences including sound effects (SFX on/off), placement guide highlights, and tap/drag input preferences.

*This data remains strictly on your local device and is never transmitted to our external servers. Clearing your application cache or uninstalling the app will reset these values.*

---

### 3. Third-Party Services & Advertisements
Block Burst Blitz is designed to be fully playable offline. In versions distributed through official app stores (such as Google Play), optional advertising (such as rewarded video clips to gain extra hints, token refills, or level revives) may be integrated using certified third-party ad networks (e.g., Google AdMob):
- **Anonymous Advertising Identifiers:** Third-party advertising partners may collect non-personal device identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID or Apple IDFA), device model, coarse geographic data, and ad interaction metrics to deliver non-intrusive, relevant advertisements and prevent ad fraud.
- These third-party ad networks operate under their respective privacy policies. You can reset or restrict your advertising identifier at any time through your mobile device system settings:
  - *Android:* Settings > Google > Ads > Reset advertising ID / Delete advertising ID
  - *iOS:* Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking

---

### 4. Children's Privacy
Block Burst Blitz is a family-friendly puzzle game suitable for audiences of all ages. We do not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or under the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). If you believe that non-compliant data collection has inadvertently occurred, please contact us immediately, and we will take prompt remedial action.

---

### 5. Security of Your Data
Because your gameplay progress and settings are maintained entirely within your device's isolated storage sandbox, your data security is governed by your own device's operating system protections.

---

### 6. Updates to This Privacy Policy
We may periodically update this Privacy Policy to reflect enhancements in the game or regulatory compliance requirements. Any revisions will be indicated by the updated "Last Updated" date at the top of this document.
